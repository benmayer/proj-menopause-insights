## Conclusion

### What it could mean
Your cycle changes can point to the hormonal transition. Longer cycles, a skipped cycle, and bigger swings between cycles fit the cycle changes described here. Your age also fits this.

Your blood results support that picture. FSH is above the usual range, oestradiol is below the usual range, and AMH is below the lab's range but still usual for age 46. Together, these values and your symptoms line up with falling ovarian hormone production.

Your sweating, sleep disruption and mood changes also fit this picture. Hot flushes or sweating, sleep trouble, and mood or concentration changes are common in this phase. TSH is within the usual range, so thyroid imbalance is less likely to explain this set of symptoms.
