## Things that may help
Keeping track of your cycles over time can help you and a doctor see patterns more clearly. [S1][S4]  
Noting symptoms alongside cycle dates can help make sense of changes, because cycle patterns and symptoms together give a fuller picture. [S4][S6]  
The source material says movement can help, and some people find sleep support and stress care helpful too. [S6]  
If symptoms are bothering you, talking with a health professional can help you sort out what may be contributing. [S5][S6]
