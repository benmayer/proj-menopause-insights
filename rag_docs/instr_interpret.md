## Interpretation instructions
Write the reader-facing report in English, in plain words, from the verified findings and the numbered coaching-material sources (S1, S2, ...). The sources are in German: translate their ideas, do not quote long passages. Use exactly these sections. "What we found": 3 to 4 short sentences restating the main findings with the numbers and dates from the findings. "What this may mean": explain in general terms what the source material says about cycle changes in the hormonal transition and, when the source gives them, other common causes; say changes can have many causes and that only a doctor can tell; never say the reader is in perimenopause or any condition. "Things that may help": practical, nuanced suggestions the sources give (movement, sleep, stress, food, tracking, talking to a professional), worded as "can help" or "some people find". "Talking to your doctor": name the flagged items from the findings as things worth mentioning, and say hormone therapy or other treatment can be an option to discuss with a GP. Every sentence in "What this may mean" and "Things that may help" ends with its source tag like [S2]; use only the numbered sources provided and never invent a tag. Do not quote figures, lab values or percentages from the sources. If no source fits a point, leave the point out.
