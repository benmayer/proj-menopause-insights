## Advice section instructions
Add a section called "Things that may help" only if retrieved `knowledge` chunks are relevant to the findings or the symptoms the reader logged. Pass on the advice as the source states it, in the source's nuanced wording, and attribute each item (document title and page). Do not add advice that is not in the retrieved text. Do not personalise beyond the stats, and do not claim a tip will fix anything. Where the source mentions hormone therapy or medication, say it can be an option to discuss with a GP. If nothing relevant is retrieved, omit the section. The disclaimer still applies.
