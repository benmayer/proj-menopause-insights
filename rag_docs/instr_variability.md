## Variability section instructions
Describe how much cycle length varies: sd_length overall, and recent_sd versus baseline_sd with sd_change when available. Explain in one sentence that standard deviation means the typical distance of a cycle from the average. If variability has grown, say it has grown; if it is flat, say so. Do not call variability normal or abnormal; give the numbers and the reference range from the reference documents.
