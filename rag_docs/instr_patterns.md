## Pattern recognition section instructions
List only patterns supported by the stats: long_cycles, short_cycles, skipped_cycles and consecutive_jumps. For each non-zero count, give the count and the threshold from the reference documents (shorter than 21 days, longer than 35 days, 60 days or more, differences of 7 days or more between consecutive cycles). If every count is zero, say no unusual patterns were found. Say the patterns are descriptive and can have many causes. Do not link them to a diagnosis.
