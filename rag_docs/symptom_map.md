section: symptom_map
source: MenoCert Webinar 2 table "Possible symptoms of hormonal imbalances by organ system" (KU-RES016, translated)

## Central nervous system and mind
Possible symptoms: difficulty concentrating (brain fog), mood swings, irritability, anxiety and inner restlessness. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: oestrogen deficiency, progesterone deficiency, thyroid disorder, high cortisol.

## Sleep regulation
Possible symptoms: trouble falling asleep, unrefreshing sleep, waking early. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: progesterone deficiency, melatonin deficiency, raised cortisol, oestrogen deficit.

## Skin and mucous membranes
Possible symptoms: dry skin, itching, redness, eczema, oily skin or acne, dry mucous membranes (vaginal, eyes, mouth). Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: oestrogen deficiency, androgen excess, underactive thyroid.

## Hair and nails
Possible symptoms: hair loss (diffuse or androgenic), facial hair growth (hirsutism), brittle nails. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: thyroid disorder, androgen dominance, oestrogen deficit.

## Breasts
Possible symptoms: tenderness, pain, lumps, tissue changes. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: relative progesterone deficiency, oestrogen dominance, progesterone dominance (with bioidentical hormone therapy in perimenopause).

## Cycle and reproduction
Possible symptoms: irregular cycle, bleeding that is too heavy or too light, absent periods (amenorrhoea), PMS, premenstrual migraine, unfulfilled wish for a child. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: LH/FSH imbalance, progesterone deficiency, relative oestrogen dominance, PCOS, oestrogen deficit.

## Sexuality and libido
Possible symptoms: loss of libido, vaginal dryness, painful sex (dyspareunia), orgasm difficulties. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: testosterone deficiency, oestrogen deficiency, progesterone deficiency.

## Cardiovascular system
Possible symptoms: palpitations, heart rhythm disturbances, blood pressure fluctuations, raised cholesterol, vascular changes. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: oestrogen deficiency, thyroid disorder, high cortisol level.

## Muscles and joints
Possible symptoms: muscle weakness, tension, cramps, joint complaints, back problems. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: testosterone deficiency, growth hormone deficiency, oestrogen deficiency.

## Bones
Possible symptoms: bone pain, osteopenia, osteoporosis, higher fracture risk. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: oestrogen deficiency, testosterone deficiency, vitamin D deficiency.

## Metabolism and weight
Possible symptoms: weight gain (especially around the belly), insulin resistance, cravings, tiredness after eating, metabolic syndrome. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: high cortisol, underactive thyroid, oestrogen deficiency.

## Digestion
Possible symptoms: bloating, constipation, diarrhoea, food intolerances, irritable-bowel-type symptoms. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: cortisol excess, oestrogen dominance (with a histamine effect), progesterone deficiency, thyroid disorder.

## Immune system
Possible symptoms: susceptibility to infections, allergic symptoms, autoimmune processes (for example Hashimoto's, lupus, rheumatism), chronic inflammation. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: cortisol imbalance, oestrogen deficiency or excess.

## Temperature regulation
Possible symptoms: hot flushes, sensitivity to cold, sweating, shivering, changing temperature sensations. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: oestrogen deficiency, thyroid disorder.

## Eyes and vision
Possible symptoms: dry eyes, vision disturbances, light sensitivity. Possible hormonal factors listed by the source: oestrogen deficiency, underactive thyroid.
